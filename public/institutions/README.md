# Institution marks

These marks appear only in the source-acknowledgement area of the internal
research demo. They are not part of the collection-media CC0/Public Domain
license, and their presence does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

Source pages were checked on 2026-08-09. The local SVGs retain the source
geometry, add an accessible title, and remove metadata or off-canvas paths that
are not needed for offline display. This repository does not claim an open
license for any institution mark.

- `cma.svg`: minified from the Cleveland Museum of Art official favicon,
  https://www.clevelandart.org/images/favicon-196x196.svg
- `aic.svg`: minified from the Art Institute of Chicago official favicon,
  https://www.artic.edu/dist/images/aic-favicon-d6b30fcfcec59c0a7126.svg
- `met.svg`: minified from the file documented at Wikimedia Commons,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_Logo.svg
  (the mark remains a trademark; the Commons page describes the text logo as
  copyright-public-domain but explicitly notes trademark restrictions). The
  Met's own account of the identity is at
  https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/brand-identity

Institution names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
